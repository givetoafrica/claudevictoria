Vertical bands

ElementPositionNotes
Headline7% to 19%Light connectors, heavy key word, centered, about 70% width
Hero19% to 67%About 60% wide, 20% margin each side, soft shadow
Pill77% to 84%One per scene, appears last
Watermarkabout 89%Mid-gray, above the caption row

Safe zones

  • Bottom 14%: author row and caption.
  • Right 12%: like, comment, share rail.
  • Sides 6%: keep text inside.
  • Turn on Show safe zones to check any scene.

Motion

  • Headline lines wipe in left to right, 0.7 s, 0.35 s apart.
  • Words start ghost gray and settle to ink.
  • Hero pops in at 0.5 s. Pill lands last, at 1.5 s.
  • One idea every 3 to 5 s. Hero swaps, not hard cuts.

Rules to keep

  • One idea and one keyword per Reel.
  • Same keyword on screen and in the caption.
  • Source-check every claim. Show the source in the caption.
  • Mock UI for tools. Real, consented images for people.
  • Say what the DM is and include an opt-out.

All tool names, comments and numbers here are samples, not facts.
