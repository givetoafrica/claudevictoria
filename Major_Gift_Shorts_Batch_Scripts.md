## SHORT 1: BREAKDOWN/EXPLAINER
**Topic: "Moves Management"**

**[HOOK — 0:00–0:03]**
"You keep hearing this word in nonprofit fundraising. Let me tell you what 'moves management' actually means."

**[DEFINITION]**
"A move is just an intentional action that advances a donor relationship — a call, a coffee, a tour, a thank-you note. Moves management is tracking those moves across five stages so no major donor ever falls through the cracks."

**[REAL WORLD EXAMPLE]**
"Instead of asking a donor for fifty thousand dollars cold, you map five conversations first: awareness, interest, cultivation, readiness, ask. Each one is a move."

**[DEEPER MEANING]**
"Why it matters: most nonprofits skip the system and jump straight to asking. That's why donors ghost you. Moves management fixes that."

## SHORT 2: A VS B COMPARISON
**Topic: "The Wrong Way vs. Right Way to Ask"**

**[HOOK — 0:00–0:03]**
"Same nonprofit. Same donor. Two completely different outcomes."

**[WRONG WAY]**
"Development director spends six months building rapport, then makes the ask cold. Donor says 'let me think about it' and disappears. Six months of work, zero gift."

**[RIGHT WAY]**
"Development director has a readiness conversation first. Three questions: 'What's your giving capacity?' 'Which program matters to you?' 'When are you ready to give?' Then the ask lands. Donor says yes."

**[THE LESSON]**
"The difference isn't charisma. It's asking at the right time with the right information."
