# THE COOK — character reference sheet

Locked reference. Generate the look **once**, then reuse it in every shot by
prepending the character block below to each shot prompt. This replaces the old
rigid ALWAYS/NEVER constraint block — consistency now comes from a single locked
description, and the calm mood comes from the shared mood block in
`storyboard.md`.

## Who she is
- Age around 35. Medium-tall, strong build, works with her hands.
- Deep brown skin.
- Headscarf: deep indigo with a small ochre geometric print, tied at the back, a
  little hair showing at the temple.
- Wrapper: same indigo and ochre cloth, worn to mid-calf.
- Top: plain rust-coloured short-sleeved blouse, sleeves pushed up.
- Barefoot, or simple worn leather sandals.
- No jewellery except one thin brass bangle, right wrist.
- Calm, unhurried, focused downward on the work.

## Reference views to generate once
Front, three-quarter, and from behind at the pot. Every video uses this sheet as
the character reference.

## Reusable character block (paste at the start of every shot prompt)
> A West African village woman, around 35, medium-tall with a strong build and
> working hands, deep brown skin. She wears a deep indigo headscarf with a small
> ochre geometric print tied at the back, a little hair showing at the temple; a
> wrapper of the same indigo-and-ochre cloth to mid-calf; a plain rust-coloured
> short-sleeved blouse with sleeves pushed up. Barefoot or in simple worn leather
> sandals. One thin brass bangle on her right wrist, no other jewellery. She is
> calm, unhurried, and focused downward on her work.
