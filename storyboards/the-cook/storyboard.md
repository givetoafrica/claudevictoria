# THE COOK — 6-shot storyboard

A quiet cooking Short. The point is village life at rest, not a recipe. It should
feel calm and relaxing — never scary. Consistency comes from the locked character
block in `character-reference.md`; the calm tone comes from the shared mood block
below, applied to every shot.

## Shared mood block (append to every shot prompt)
> Soft natural daylight with warm low firelight, gentle steady rain in the
> background. Slow, unhurried camera. Warm earthy colours, cozy and peaceful, a
> gentle documentary feel. No dramatic shadows, no harsh contrast, no eerie or
> horror mood, no sudden movement, no fast cuts.

Each shot below is a ready-to-run prompt for the Gathos video workflow
(`.github/workflows/gathos-generate-video.yml`). Shots 2–6 already include the
character block; shot 1 has no person. Every shot ends with the shared mood cues.

---

## Shot 1 · Rain off the roof
*Framing: no one — pure atmosphere hook.*

A close, peaceful shot of steady rain running off the edge of a village roof,
water falling in a soft ribbon, warm firelight glowing softly out of focus
behind. No people. Meditative, the quiet rhythm of the rain. Soft natural
daylight with warm low firelight, gentle steady rain. Slow, unhurried camera.
Warm earthy colours, cozy and peaceful, a gentle documentary feel. No dramatic
shadows, no harsh contrast, no eerie or horror mood, no sudden movement, no fast
cuts.

## Shot 2 · She carries the pot to the fire
*Framing: full figure, from behind or the side.*

A West African village woman, around 35, medium-tall with a strong build and
working hands, deep brown skin. She wears a deep indigo headscarf with a small
ochre geometric print tied at the back, a little hair showing at the temple; a
wrapper of the same indigo-and-ochre cloth to mid-calf; a plain rust-coloured
short-sleeved blouse with sleeves pushed up. Barefoot or in simple worn leather
sandals. One thin brass bangle on her right wrist, no other jewellery. She is
calm, unhurried, and focused downward on her work. Full-figure shot from behind
or the side: she carries a large black cooking pot with both hands toward a low
cooking fire under an open shelter, moving slowly and steadily, rain falling
softly around the open sides. Soft natural daylight with warm low firelight,
gentle steady rain. Slow, unhurried camera. Warm earthy colours, cozy and
peaceful, a gentle documentary feel. No dramatic shadows, no harsh contrast, no
eerie or horror mood, no sudden movement, no fast cuts.
