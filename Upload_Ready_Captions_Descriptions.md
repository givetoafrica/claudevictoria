## SHORT 1: "What Is Moves Management?"

### TITLE
**What Is Moves Management? (Nonprofit Fundraising Explained)**

### DESCRIPTION
A move is an intentional action that advances a donor relationship. Moves management is tracking those moves across five stages so no major donor falls through the cracks.

Instead of asking donors cold, you map five conversations: awareness, interest, cultivation, readiness, ask. Each one is a move.

Most nonprofits skip the system and jump straight to asking. That's why donors ghost you. Moves management fixes that.

## SHORT 2: "The Wrong Way vs. Right Way to Ask"

### TITLE
**How NOT to Ask for a Major Gift (Biggest Mistake)**

### DESCRIPTION
Same nonprofit. Same donor. Two completely different outcomes.

**Wrong way:** Spend six months building rapport, then ask cold. Donor disappears. Six months of work, zero gift.

**Right way:** Have a readiness conversation first. Ask three questions: "What's your giving capacity?" "Which program matters to you?" "When are you ready to give?" Then the ask lands.

The difference isn't charisma. It's asking at the right time with the right information.
