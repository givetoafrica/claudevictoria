## Why viewers are bored
"Another survival story" is a commodity. Viewers don't leave over the topic. They leave because they know the shape: a setup, a struggle, a rescue. To wow them, change the structure, not just the subject.

## Five dramatic levers (use at least two per video)
1. **Cold open at the worst second.** The first 15 seconds sit in the crisis (a cliff edge, an empty canteen). Then rewind: "Nine days earlier, he had no idea."
2. **The live clock.** A running on-screen timer (Hour 4, Day 3, Day 9) with the body's condition updating beside it. The viewer feels time running out.
3. **The decision.** Build each video around one impossible choice and make the viewer pick before the reveal. This also drives comments.
4. **Survival science beats.** "What your body does at hour 72 without water." Facts that make people say "I didn't know that" get shared.
5. **Series, not one-offs.** A repeatable name viewers return for: *Last 24 Hours*, *The Decision*, *Day Zero to Day Rescue*.

## Packaging rules
- **Thumbnail:** one face or one object in extreme close-up, one number ("9 DAYS"), 3 words or fewer.
- **Title:** a stake plus a number ("He Had 9 Days of Water He Didn't Have"). No fake claims. Make the title match the video, or retention collapses.
- **First 30 seconds:** no logo sting, no "welcome back", no "like and subscribe".
