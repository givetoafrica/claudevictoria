Year-End Appeal in 30 Days

The Nonprofits Daily checklist: one week at a time

WEEK 1 · PLAN

Set one goal: a dollar amount and a donor count
Pick one story, one need, one ask
Pull your list and split it three ways: current donors, lapsed donors, warm prospects
Choose your channels (letter, email, social, a personal call list) and only what your team can finish

WEEK 2 · WRITE

Lead with a person, not your organization
Name one real outcome and say what a gift does
Make one clear ask with one clear link
Thank donors before you ask again
Write the letter first, then adapt it to email, social posts and a call script
Get one person outside your team to read it

WEEK 3 · LAUNCH

Mail the letter so it lands before Giving Tuesday (December 1, 2026); send your first email the same week
Make personal calls and write handwritten notes to your top donors
Post your story on social with your donation link
Test your donation page on a phone
Confirm receipts send automatically

WEEK 4 · FOLLOW UP

Send a reminder to everyone who hasn't responded, and a different message to those who have
Thank every donor within 48 hours and tell them what their gift will do
Send a deadline email on December 30 and 31
Track results: gifts, donors, and which channel worked
