# Victoria's writing voice (NonprofitsDaily)

Source: her real Instagram captions read through RobinReach (Oct 2026), not invented. Update this file when she sends more scripts.

## Pattern in her top posts
- **Opens with a plain statement or a title line.** Examples: "Why first-time donors don't come back. It's usually not because they stopped caring." / "Planned giving, in plain English." / "Don't send your year-end appeal yet." / "Write these three rules before you touch your reserve."
- **Three numbered points**, one short sentence each. Concrete nouns, no jargon.
- **A picture to hold onto**: "Picture a donor who has given for years..." / "Picture a small nonprofit with a little money saved."
- **One closing principle**: "Funders don't reward the greatest need. They reward the clearest plan."
- **A question to the viewer** near the end: "Which of these three have you seen in your own proposals?"
- **Standard sign-off:** "If you're a nonprofit leader who wants to grow, let's do it together. Follow NonprofitsDaily for more."
- **Hashtags:** about six: #nonprofit, two topic tags, #fundraising or #grants, #nonprofitleadership, #nonprofitsdaily.
- **Teaching examples are labeled**: "Second Chances Austin, our teaching example" / "This is a teaching scenario, not a real funder's note."
- **Legal/financial topics point to an expert**: planned giving says to invite donors to talk with their own attorney or advisor.

## Spoken version for reels and longs
- Say the hook in the first two seconds. Plain words, short sentences, warm and direct. Read it aloud.
- Keep the same three-point structure: hook, three points, one principle, one question or follow line.
- End reels with "Follow for more nonprofit tips." (default). Do not use a comment-keyword or checklist call to action unless Victoria asks for one.

## Do not
- Mention Give to Africa on NonprofitsDaily.
- State a statistic without a source she can check. Put the source on screen.
- Present invented examples as real. Label them.

## Evidence limits
Engagement scores seen: first-time donors 128, planned giving 96, grant rejection reasons 47, year-end appeal 34. Small sample, scores come from RobinReach and are not reach.
