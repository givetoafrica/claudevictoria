## PICTORY.AI IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

### Caption Settings:
- **Font:** Sans-serif (Arial, Helvetica, or similar), white
- **Size:** 28-36pt (adjust based on Pictory preset scaling)
- **Contrast:** White (#FFFFFF) on semi-transparent black bars or Nonprofit Daily brand color (#1F4788)
- **Timing:** 1.5–7 seconds per caption (typically 3-5 seconds for standard reading)
- **Placement:** Center or upper-center for primary messages; upper-left/right for secondary overlays
- **Animation:** Fade-in for standard captions; slide-in or pop for emphasis overlays

### Text Overlay Strategy:
- Use overlays (not captions) for numbers, stats, and key phrases
- Animate entrance on key hook moments (0:08, 0:25, 2:15, etc.)
- Keep overlays visible 3-5 seconds before fading
- Use brand color (#E8A87C) for accent bars or emphasis elements

### B-Roll / Visual Guidance:
- Open with authentic (not stock) footage where possible
- Use 1.5–2 second cuts in opening (energetic); 2–3 second cuts in teaching sections
- Maintain professional, warm tone throughout (nonprofit professionals watching)
- Include real people (staff, beneficiaries, donors) for credibility
- Avoid overly dramatic transitions; stick to simple fades and cuts

### Pacing Targets:
- First 15 seconds: Hook + promise
- Every 2-3 minutes: Re-hook or pattern break
- Chapter transitions: Quick text overlay + visual shift
- Audio: Match voiceover rhythm; avoid monotone delivery

## DELIVERY NOTES FOR RECORDING

This script is written for a knowledgeable nonprofit professional speaking to peers. Tone: Expert, practical, encouraging—not talking down to the audience. Energy should increase at re-hook moments (marked in script). Pace should match the teaching beats: slower on frameworks, faster on examples.

**Target speaking pace:** 140-150 words per minute (this script ≈ 2,700 words = 18-19 minutes if read at standard pace; editing cuts it to 10-12 minutes in Pictory).

**Recording tip:** Record in sections by chapter; pause between chapters. This makes editing easier in Pictory.ai's text-sync workflow.
